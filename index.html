This is not our end,
this is just process.

She was drowning in the dark. He stood still, carrying a pain heavier than the night.

She waited…
with tears that never stopped falling.

He fought the whole world
with a broken body and a smiling face.

All my tears
have been used up.

He sat there bleeding, still smiling, because some loves demand everything.

And after everything…
we finally became one.

Not because the world allowed it. But because we refused to let go.

"We're just two souls who kept walking toward each other, through every world that told us not to."
